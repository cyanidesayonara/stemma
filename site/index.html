How it works

  1. Import an MP3, WAV or FLAC file, or paste a YouTube link.
  2. Separate it into 2, 4 or 6 stems. It all happens on your PC; the first time, stemma downloads the models it needs.
  3. Practice. Mute your part, loop the hard bars, slow them down, change the key, and record your take over the band.

Your songs stay yours

Separation runs on your own machine. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account and no telemetry, and once the models are downloaded stemma works offline. Two-stem separation uses your GPU when Windows supports it, and falls back to the CPU when it does not.
